We began with the goal of zero tailpipe emissions and built everything around it—solving the logistical challenges from the ground up. No ageing, polluting fleets to work around. No compromises. Just the latest technology, a purpose-built electric fleet, and a clear view of London’s future as a carbon net-zero city by 2050.\n\nCo-founders Ollie and Joe bring over twenty years of combined operations experience—managing large teams, running fleets, and, crucially, getting their hands dirty. Together, they’ve designed and implemented workflow, scheduling, and fleet systems for some of London’s biggest logistics operators—now bringing all that know-how to Zhero."
